Stanley R. Petrovich

Stanley R. Petrovich, 91, of Valparaiso, entered into rest on September 15, 2026. Born January 9, 1935 in East Chicago, he served in the U.S. Army and later owned a hardware store in Portage.

Stan was preceded in death by his wife, Dorothy J. Petrovich, in 2021. He is survived by his daughters Anne (Paul) Kerr of Portage and Linda Petrovich of Chicago, IL; and his nephew Michael Petrovich of Valparaiso.

Arrangements by Testcrest Funeral Home, Valparaiso.
