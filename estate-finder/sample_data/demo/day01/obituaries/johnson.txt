Mary A. Johnson, 79, of Gary, passed away Sunday, September 20, 2026. She was born March 3, 1947 in Memphis, Tennessee and moved to Gary in 1965.

She is survived by her loving husband of 55 years, Robert L. Johnson; son James (Tina) Johnson of Houston, TX; daughter Karen Johnson of Gary; and many grandchildren.

Services entrusted to Sampleview Funeral Home.
