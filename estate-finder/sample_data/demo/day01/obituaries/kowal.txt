Walter E. "Wally" Kowal

Walter E. "Wally" Kowal, 84, of Highland, passed away peacefully on Monday, September 28, 2026. He was born on May 2, 1942 in Gary to the late Stanley and Anna Kowal. Wally worked 38 years at the steel mill and was a proud member of St. James parish.

He was preceded in death by his beloved wife of 51 years, Helen M. (Nowak) Kowal, and by his brother Edward.

Wally is survived by his son Walter Jr. (Linda) Kowal of Tempe, AZ; daughter Susan (Mark) Brennan of Munster; and five grandchildren.

Visitation will be held at Exampleton Funeral Home, Highland.
